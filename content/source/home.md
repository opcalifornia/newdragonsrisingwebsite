## New Dragons Rising: Journey through the Mestizo Method

**Image:** `8T0A1630.JPG`

### Collaborative Martial Arts Innovation

New Dragons Rising was created by Grand Master Rudy Torres, renowned for his invaluable contributions to martial arts. The inception of this initiative was driven by the vision of GM Torres, alongside friends, family, and fellow martial artists. Their collective effort led to the formation of a unique method known as the "Mestizo Method," which seamlessly integrates various martial arts disciplines, with a significant emphasis on Esgrima, Kali, and Arnis.

The Mestizo Method serves as an invitation to martial artists and schools inspired by GM Torres' son, Rudy Torres Jr., a martial artist extraordinaire. Rudy Torres Jr., under the tutelage of his father, embraced the philosophy of learning from everyone and everything, including the intricate techniques of Esgrima, the fluid movements of Kali, and the practical applications of Arnis. This ethos forms the cornerstone of New Dragons Rising.

The driving force behind New Dragons Rising is the commitment to foster growth, experience, diversity, and knowledge within the martial arts community. The journey embarked upon by GM Torres and Rudy Torres Jr. is one of continuous learning and unification of different martial arts practices — particularly Esgrima, Kali, and Arnis. This initiative aims to transcend traditional boundaries and create a cohesive environment where martial artists can share their expertise and learn from one another.

### Origins and Vision

Grand Master Rudy Torres, a prominent figure in martial arts, has always been passionate about the art's evolution and the collaborative fusion of different styles. The creation of New Dragons Rising was born out of a desire to bring together diverse martial arts forms, such as Esgrima, Kali, and Arnis, under one umbrella, fostering a culture of mutual respect and shared knowledge.

The Mestizo Method, which lies at the heart of New Dragons Rising, embodies the concept of blending various martial arts techniques into a unified practice. This method not only celebrates the rich heritage of each discipline, especially Esgrima, Kali, and Arnis, but also encourages practitioners to broaden their horizons and explore new techniques.

### The Role of Rudy Torres Jr.

Rudy Torres Jr., the son of GM Torres, has played a pivotal role in shaping the vision of New Dragons Rising. His extraordinary skills and dedication to martial arts have been instrumental in inspiring others to embrace the Mestizo Method. Rudy Jr.'s journey in martial arts, guided by his father's wisdom, exemplifies the spirit of learning and adaptability.

Under GM Torres' guidance, Rudy Jr. has mastered the art of learning from everyone and everything, including the techniques of Esgrima, Kali, and Arnis — a principle that is now embedded in the DNA of New Dragons Rising. His ability to integrate diverse techniques and philosophies has greatly contributed to the evolution of the Mestizo Method.

Unfortunately, and a great loss to us all, young master Rudy Torres Jr. was taken from us in a devastating accident. Though he may be physically gone, his spirit, perseverance, inspiration and contribution to life and the martial arts will forever live in perpetuity.

God bless and keep you.

### David A. Aviles

**Image:** `Untitled design (4).png`

David A. Aviles was born in the Bronx and raised in Spanish Harlem and Brooklyn. His journey into martial arts began at the age of 12 under the guidance of Arturo Aviles, where he trained in the discipline of GoJu Kai. This foundational training set the stage for what would become a lifelong dedication to martial arts.

David's commitment to his craft continued through his military service in the Air Force, where he had the opportunity to train with the Republic of Korean Army during his deployment to Vietnam. This period of training further honed his skills and broadened his martial arts perspective.

Upon returning, David continued his martial arts education under several esteemed Grandmasters. He trained with GM Gerald Gaylord in Ketsugen and Albert Andino in Meibukan GoJu Ryu, and furthered his expertise under GM Rudy Torres and GM Vincent Tinga. His dedication and proficiency were recognized as he achieved the rank of 4th degree under the Menehune system with GM Torres.

In addition to his GoJu Kai training, David is also a proud practitioner of Esgrima and Arnis Kali. He has embraced the teachings of New Dragons and the Mestizo Method, which have enriched his martial arts repertoire and philosophy.

David A. Aviles' martial arts journey is a testament to his enduring passion, discipline, and respect for the traditions and innovations within the field. His story is one of perseverance and dedication, inspiring those who follow in his footsteps.

### Tony Burgos

Tony Burgos was born to Maximo Quismondo Burgos and Rosie Moran Burgos. His father, born in Santo Domingo, Iloko, Sur, was a professional boxer. A field worker when he wasn't fighting, Maximo was a dedicated family man. Married to Rosie, Tony's mother and Maximo's love of his life. Rosie, a dedicated wife and mother, also a nurse's aide, sharing her time between her beloved family and her willingness to help others. Their love story was one of faithful, unconditional love.

Tony is a lifelong resident of Stockton who believes in the adage, "There's no place like home." He is deeply passionate about his home in Stockton. Tony started training in the Martial Arts when he was 12 years old and is still a practitioner till today. Mr. Burgos is also a published poet, which is just one of his many passions. Tony is also an organ donor. Willing to sacrifice for others no matter the price, Mr. Burgos exhibits what very few people can even imagine. He deeply cares for others, sharing his love and compassion physically, emotionally, and spiritually.

Mr. Burgos also extends his expertise in his quest for historical and cultural truth.

We are proud and thankful for Tony Burgos for extending his talents, beliefs and expertise to "New Dragons Rising" the "Mestizo Method".

### Tony Ulep — A Life of Service and Artistic Expression

Tony Ulep, a Chief Warrant Officer and a martial arts practitioner, has traversed the globe, immersing himself in diverse cultures through his passion for photography. His portfolio is a rich tapestry that includes architecture, landscapes, flora, and both sunrises and sunsets, capturing the beauty of the world around him.

A combat veteran, Tony finds solace and therapy in his photography, using it as a tool to manage the stress of combat and as a medium to convey his experiences. His illustrious military career in the Marine Corps spanned from 1988 to 2017, with 15 years as an enlisted man followed by 14 years as an officer. Tony served with distinction during Operation Enduring Freedom in Afghanistan and Operation Iraqi Freedom, deploying on three separate occasions.

In addition to his military service, Tony is a committed father and family man who successfully balanced his duties with a significant civilian role. From 1996 to 2021, he worked with the City of Sacramento as the Department of Parks Maintenance Manager. In this capacity, he oversaw an annual budget of 25 million dollars, managed 225 parks covering 4000 acres, and supervised 130 full-time employees.

We are truly fortunate to have such a talented and dedicated veteran like Tony Ulep sharing his expertise and artistic vision to elevate the goals of New Dragons Rising and the Mestizo Method.

### David Betts — Esteemed Sergeant at Arms

David Betts, born in November 1968, was raised in Stockton, California. Of Puerto Rican and Caucasian descent, Betts has worked in the security industry for 10 years and takes immense pride in his mixed heritage as a Mestizo. He has held the position of Sergeant at Arms in various organizations, showcasing his unwavering commitment to proper procedure and responsibility.

Betts's dedication to his duties has earned him the role of overseeing the safety and security of GM Torres, as well as other members of New Dragons Rising (NDR). His expertise in martial arts, both in mind and spirit, further exemplifies his commitment to security.

David's loyalty is evidenced by his 20-year tenure with GM Torres and his continued drive for success within NDR. His disciplined approach and relentless pursuit of excellence make him a true asset to the organization and a respected figure in the security industry.

### Master Mark Cantu — From Boxing to the Mestizo Method

Master Mark Cantu, born on August 3, 1972, has carved a remarkable path in the world of martial arts. His journey began informally when he started training in boxing and the KAJUKENBO style under the guidance of his uncle and Grand Master John Valdivia. The early exposure to these disciplines laid a solid foundation for his future endeavors.

Seeking to broaden his skills, Master Mark trained in Tae Kwon Do under the mentorship of Grand Master Robin Taberna. This period of training enhanced his expertise, but his insatiable quest for knowledge and improvement led him back to his uncle and Grand Master John Valdivia, as well as Grand Master Saldana. It was under their tutelage that he achieved the prestigious rank of Black Belt in 2005.

Not content to stop there, Master Mark's journey took him into the realms of Jiu Jitsu and MMA-style fighting. His commitment to mastering these arts was evident through his 12 years of competition. Yet, his passion for his original art, Kajukembo, drew him back to its reformed version, KajuKenbo Karate, as developed by Master Valdivia. His dedication and skill earned him the esteemed 6th-degree black belt.

For the past 3.5 years, Master Mark has continued his relentless pursuit of martial arts excellence under the guidance of Grand Master Rudy Torres. This phase of his training has expanded his knowledge into Esgrima and allowed him to contribute significantly to the "Mestizo Method" of martial arts.

Master Mark Cantu's journey is a testament to his unwavering dedication and love for martial arts, constantly seeking to learn, grow, and share his expertise with others.
