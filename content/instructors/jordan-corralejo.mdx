My name is Jordan Corralejo, and I have earned my green belt under our esteemed Grandmaster Rudy Torres. Over the past decade, my training under Grandmaster Rudy Torres, though intermittent, has allowed me to compete as an esgrimador and profoundly shaped my martial arts journey. Throughout these ten years, I have competed in numerous tournaments, showcasing my skills and dedication to my art.

In the past two years, I have been a proud member of a team that achieved the "Las Vegas grand champion" title in team (padded stick) competitions. This accomplishment speaks volumes about our collective effort and harmonious teamwork.

Besides my passion for martial arts, I am also a skilled graphic designer. I have had the privilege of creating and designing the logo and visual identity for New Dragons Rising. Currently, I am associate director of marketing and social media efforts, combining my artistic talents with my commitment to promoting the association's values and achievements.
