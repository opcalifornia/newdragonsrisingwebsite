Grandmaster Rudy Torres is the founder of New Dragons Rising and the creator of the **Mestizo Method** — a synthesis of Filipino martial arts disciplines built with a significant emphasis on Esgrima, Kali, and Arnis.

New Dragons Rising was born from GM Torres' vision, developed alongside friends, family, and fellow martial artists, to bring diverse martial arts forms together under one roof and foster a culture of mutual respect and shared knowledge. That work continues today through New Dragons Rising's collaboration with the American Filipino Martial Arts Association.

GM Torres has personally guided the training of instructors across New Dragons Rising, including Datu Elsa Torres, David A. Aviles, Master Mark Cantu, Jordan Corralejo, and David Betts — several of whom have trained under him for decades.

The Mestizo Method was shaped in part alongside his son, Rudy Torres Jr., whose own journey and legacy are honored on [In Memoriam: Rudy Torres Jr.](/about/in-memoriam).
