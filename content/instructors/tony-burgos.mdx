Tony Burgos was born to Maximo Quismondo Burgos and Rosie Moran Burgos. His father, born in Santo Domingo, Iloko, Sur, was a professional boxer. A field worker when he wasn't fighting, Maximo was a dedicated family man. Married to Rosie, Tony's mother and Maximo's love of his life. Rosie, a dedicated wife and mother, also a nurse's aide, sharing her time between her beloved family and her willingness to help others. Their love story was one of faithful, unconditional love.

Tony is a lifelong resident of Stockton who believes in the adage, "There's no place like home." He is deeply passionate about his home in Stockton. Tony started training in the martial arts when he was 12 years old and is still a practitioner today. Mr. Burgos is also a published poet, which is just one of his many passions. Tony is also an organ donor — willing to sacrifice for others no matter the price, exhibiting what very few people can even imagine. He deeply cares for others, sharing his love and compassion physically, emotionally, and spiritually.

Mr. Burgos also extends his expertise in his quest for historical and cultural truth. New Dragons Rising and the Mestizo Method are proud and thankful for Tony Burgos extending his talents, beliefs, and expertise.
