Her journey in this disciplined art form has not only honed her physical prowess but also instilled a sense of resilience and dedication that she brings into every aspect of her life.

In her professional life, she serves as a dedicated employee of Caltrans, showcasing her unwavering commitment to public service.

Mrs. Torres is also a dynamic community and events organizer, tirelessly advocating for children's welfare and community involvement. Her efforts have fostered a sense of unity and purpose among those she touches, making her an indispensable asset to the New Dragons Rising community.

In all her endeavors, Mrs. Torres brings a unique blend of strength, compassion, and leadership. Her support for Grand Master Torres and the New Dragons Rising family is unparalleled, as she seamlessly integrates her myriad of talents to uplift and inspire those around her.
