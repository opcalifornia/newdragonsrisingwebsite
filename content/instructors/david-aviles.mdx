David A. Aviles was born in the Bronx and raised in Spanish Harlem and Brooklyn. His journey into martial arts began at the age of 12 under the guidance of Arturo Aviles, where he trained in the discipline of GoJu Kai. This foundational training set the stage for what would become a lifelong dedication to martial arts.

David's commitment to his craft continued through his military service in the Air Force, where he had the opportunity to train with the Republic of Korean Army during his deployment to Vietnam. This period of training further honed his skills and broadened his martial arts perspective.

Upon returning, David continued his martial arts education under several esteemed Grandmasters. He trained with GM Gerald Gaylord in Ketsugen and Albert Andino in Meibukan GoJu Ryu, and furthered his expertise under GM Rudy Torres and GM Vincent Tinga. His dedication and proficiency were recognized as he achieved the rank of 4th degree under the Menehune system with GM Torres.

In addition to his GoJu Kai training, David is also a proud practitioner of Esgrima and Arnis Kali. He has embraced the teachings of New Dragons Rising and the Mestizo Method, which have enriched his martial arts repertoire and philosophy.
