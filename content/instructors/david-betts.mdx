David Betts, born in November 1968, was raised in Stockton, California. Of Puerto Rican and Caucasian descent, Betts has worked in the security industry for 10 years and takes immense pride in his mixed heritage as a Mestizo. He has held the position of Sergeant at Arms in various organizations, showcasing his unwavering commitment to proper procedure and responsibility.

Betts's dedication to his duties has earned him the role of overseeing the safety and security of GM Torres, as well as other members of New Dragons Rising. His expertise in martial arts, both in mind and spirit, further exemplifies his commitment to security.

David's loyalty is evidenced by his 20-year tenure with GM Torres and his continued drive for success within New Dragons Rising. His disciplined approach and relentless pursuit of excellence make him a true asset to the organization and a respected figure in the security industry.
