Accessibility Statement

Attorney review pending. The old site never had real language here (see content/source/CLIENT-AUDIT.md) — what follows is a complete draft written for New Dragons Rising’s actual business (Stockton, CA; the services, products, and data practices described on this site), not generic filler. It has not yet been reviewed by a licensed attorney and should not be treated as final until it is.

Last updated: [effective date to be added at launch]

New Dragons Rising is committed to making https://www.newdragonsrising.com usable by everyone, including people using assistive technology such as screen readers, keyboard-only navigation, or voice control.

Conformance Target

This site is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA.

Measures We’ve Taken

  • Full keyboard navigability with a visible focus state throughout
  • Text contrast checked against both black and surface backgrounds, meeting AA thresholds
  • All animation and 3D effects respect your device’s reduced-motion setting
  • Semantic headings, landmarks, and labeled form fields throughout
  • Accessible names on icon-only controls (cart, menu, account, social links)

Known Limitations

We’re not aware of every limitation until real content and real users test it. Two known gaps as of this writing:

  • Real photography is still being added; descriptive alt text will be written for each image as it’s supplied.
  • Video captions for training module lessons will be added once video hosting is connected.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this site, please tell us — we want to fix it. Contact newdragonsrisingtek@gmail.com or (209) 507-9630 with the page and a description of the issue, and we’ll respond and work to address it.
